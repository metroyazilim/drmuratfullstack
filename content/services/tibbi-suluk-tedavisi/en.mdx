## What Is Hirudotherapy?

Hirudotherapy is a traditional treatment method based on bringing the leech — which carries many therapeutic active substances — into contact with points on the skin determined by the physician, so that these substances act both locally and through the bloodstream.

## Where Do the Leeches Come From?

The leeches used must be obtained from production facilities approved by the Ministry of Agriculture. This is a basic condition for the safety of the procedure.

## Procedure After Treatment

At the end of the procedure, the leeches are disposed of in accordance with medical waste regulations. Each leech must be used only once, for a single patient.
