## What Is Ozone Therapy?

Ozone applications are among the practices assessed within the scope of traditional and complementary medicine under Ministry of Health regulations. Dr. Murat Irmak has completed Ministry-approved training and certification programmes in this field.

## Who Is It Suitable For?

Suitability for ozone applications varies from person to person. No treatment decision is made before the physician has reviewed the person health status, current medication and the appropriate indication.

## How Does the Process Work?

Medical history is taken at the initial consultation and expectations are discussed. If considered appropriate, a session plan is drawn up individually and the process is followed up with check-up appointments.
