## What Is Dermapen?

Dermapen is one of the methods that helps address a range of skin concerns. Also known as microneedling, it is a skin renewal method that triggers the body natural renewal mechanism.

## How Does It Work?

It aims to trigger collagen production in the skin and open micro-channels using micro-needles. Needles that enter and leave the skin rapidly and vertically activate the skin healing mechanisms.

## How Long Does It Take?

The whole procedure takes approximately 20 to 40 minutes.
