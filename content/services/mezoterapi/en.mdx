## What Is Mesotherapy?

Mesotherapy is a treatment method first used in France in 1950. In recent years it has become widely used in cosmetic dermatology.

## How Is It Applied?

Mesotherapy means injecting certain preparations into the middle layer of the skin in the area of concern using micro-needles. Special mixtures of hyaluronic acid, antioxidants, vitamins, minerals, amino acids and other materials are prepared according to the skin concern.

## What Changes in the Skin?

Delivering these substances directly into the skin increases collagen and elastin synthesis and contributes to skin renewal.
