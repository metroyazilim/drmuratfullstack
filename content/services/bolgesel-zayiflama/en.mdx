## What Is Body Contouring?

Fat that creates a disproportionate appearance tends to collect on the abdomen, hips, buttocks and upper legs. For this kind of localised fat, where diet programmes do not always deliver results, body contouring methods are increasingly preferred.

## How Is the Number of Sessions Decided?

The number of sessions is determined once a specialist has assessed your body. There is no single standard programme; the area, the distribution of fat and your expectations are considered together.

## What Makes Results Last?

For people with a weight problem, the treatment may need to be planned alongside a diet programme. This helps the effect of body shaping methods last longer.
