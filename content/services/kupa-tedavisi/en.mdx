## What Is Cupping Therapy?

Cupping therapy, popularly known as hijama, is one of the widely used detox methods. It is a traditional treatment method that creates local suction with the aim of supporting the person's blood circulation.

## Regulatory Context

The method is listed by the Turkish Ministry of Health among traditional and complementary medicine practices. It is performed by a trained physician under clinical conditions.

## Historical Background

With a history spanning thousands of years, cupping appears in Egyptian, Ancient Roman, Chinese, Arab (al-hijama) and Greek medicine. Galen and Hippocrates, regarded among the founders of Western medicine, also made use of cupping and shared their observations in their written works.
