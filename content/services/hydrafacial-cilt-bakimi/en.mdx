## What Is Hydrafacial?

Hydrafacial skin renovation is one of the more advanced routes to skin renewal, particularly for pigmentation and acne concerns. The method should be performed with devices approved by the FDA.

## Which Areas Are Treated?

All areas can be addressed comfortably with Hydrafacial. The most frequently requested areas are the face, neck and décolleté.

## Comfort During Treatment

Hydrafacial is not a painful procedure; it generally causes no discomfort and is planned as a comfortable care session.
