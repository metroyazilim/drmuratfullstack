## How Is Pigmentation Treatment Planned?

Treatment for skin discolouration can vary considerably depending on the cause. If an underlying health condition lies behind the marks, the physician assesses that condition. Addressing the underlying cause often helps resolve the associated skin problems as well.

## There Is No Single Method

There is no single treatment method for pigmentation. Depending on the person's skin type and the depth of the marks, one or several methods may be used together.

## When Should You Not Wait?

If the underlying cause is a serious skin condition, it is important to consult a physician as soon as possible.
