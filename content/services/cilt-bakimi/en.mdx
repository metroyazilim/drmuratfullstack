## What Is Skin Care?

Skin care is a set of practices that support the integrity of the skin, improve its appearance and ease skin conditions. It can also include nutrition, avoiding excessive sunlight and the appropriate use of moisturisers.

## Why Is It Done Regularly?

A bright and smooth appearance starts with the right skin care. Care aimed at maintaining the appearance of the skin covers treatments that renew it by clearing away the dead layer that builds up on the surface.

## How Is the Protocol Decided?

There is no single standard routine. Skin type, current skin conditions and expectations are assessed together to create an individual care plan.
