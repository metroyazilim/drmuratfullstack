## Quality Policy

The quality policy of Dr. Murat Irmak Clinic is to provide accurate diagnosis and treatment through evidence-based medical practice, to prevent errors, to improve the care process, to increase the satisfaction of both patients and staff, and to reach the best possible health outcomes.
