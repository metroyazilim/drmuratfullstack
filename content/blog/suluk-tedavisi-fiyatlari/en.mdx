## What Shapes the Process?

There is no single standard plan in medicinal leech therapy. Examination findings, the treatment area, the number of sessions and the person overall health status are assessed together to determine the process.

## Why Does Examination Come First?

Medical history is taken before the procedure; medicines in use, bleeding tendency and existing conditions are reviewed. No session is planned before suitability has been assessed by the physician.

## Materials and Safety

Sourcing leeches from approved production facilities and using each leech only once are basic safety conditions of the process. Medical waste procedures are applied at the end of the session.

## Follow-Up

Aftercare advice is shared after the procedure and a check-up appointment is planned where considered necessary.
