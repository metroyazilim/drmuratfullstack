## Where Cupping Sits

Cupping therapy is a method listed by the Turkish Ministry of Health among traditional and complementary medicine practices. It aims to support blood circulation by creating local suction.

## Historical Background

With a history spanning thousands of years, cupping appears in Egyptian, Ancient Roman, Chinese, Arab and Greek medicine. Pioneers of Western medicine such as Galen and Hippocrates also made use of the method and recorded their observations in their works.

## Assessment Before Treatment

It is not a suitable method for everyone. No treatment decision should be made without assessing medical history, medicines in use and the current condition.

## Clinical Conditions

The procedure should be performed by a trained physician under sterile clinical conditions. Aftercare advice and the follow-up process are planned from the outset.
