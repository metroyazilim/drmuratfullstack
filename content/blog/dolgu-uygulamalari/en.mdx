## Anatomical Assessment

The first step in filler treatments is assessing the area anatomically. A treatment plan is not created before establishing where volume loss has occurred and to what extent.

## How Are Volume Needs Determined?

Volume needs vary from person to person. Age, skin structure, bone support and existing facial proportions are assessed together to determine the appropriate amount.

## Preserving Facial Proportions

The aim is not to change an area in isolation, but to plan within a whole that preserves facial proportions. This approach is decisive for how natural the result looks.

## Planning for a Natural Result

Gradual planning helps keep the result controllable. The assessment is repeated at the check-up appointment after treatment.
