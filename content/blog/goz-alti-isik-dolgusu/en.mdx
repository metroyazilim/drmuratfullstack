Given the hours we spend in front of monitors and the constant attention we give to phone and television screens, under-eye bags, dark circles and hollows are a concern for a great many people, women and men alike.

The most frequently preferred treatment for the under-eye area is the light filler. It aims to address the problems seen around the eyes. Light fillers usually use hyaluronic acid, a substance that also occurs in our bodies, although other materials may be used as well.

<Callout title="Good to know">
Under-eye light filler carried out at an earlier age is reported to help delay the dark circles and bags that develop after the age of 30.
</Callout>

## How Is the Procedure Performed?

This treatment requires no surgery and can easily be carried out even during a lunch break. Physicians use fine-tipped filler injection instruments for it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Examination">
    A detailed examination is needed before treatment so that the severity of the problem and factors such as skin tone can be established.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Anaesthesia">
    After the assessment, local anaesthesia is applied around the eye and left to take effect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hygiene and preparation">
    Hygiene is critical in under-eye light filler as in every medical procedure; the area around and beneath the eyes is cleaned carefully, then gone over once more with a tonic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Application">
    The light filler is applied in measured amounts to the affected areas. Precision is maintained throughout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finishing">
    Once the filler is placed, the under-eye area is cleaned again with tonic. Creams are applied to prevent redness and a cold compress is used to minimise any redness that does occur.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why Do Under-Eye Hollows Form?

The barrier area beneath the eye loosens with genetic predisposition or advancing age and is pushed forward by the fat pad. In later years the bone structure of the face thins and the bone naturally recedes. This is why hollows can appear under the eyes.

## The Effect of Light Filler on Under-Eye Bags

After the procedure the active ingredient, hyaluronic acid, begins to work and the production of elastin and collagen increases. Under-eye problems can be seen to diminish as a result. The most important quality of hyaluronic acid is that it carries water to the skin and holds it there for a long time. This preserves the skin's moisture balance, rejuvenating the under-eye area and giving it a brighter appearance.

Under-eye light filler is a procedure that takes 15-20 minutes.

## How Long Does It Last?

The most common question is whether the filler is permanent. It is not; how long it lasts depends on the content of the filler material used. The person's skin structure also affects this. Although a single figure is hard to give, light filler applied for under-eye shadows lasts around 9 months.

## When Is Under-Eye Filler Preferred?

- In people with dark circles and grey dullness around and beneath the eyes
- For under-eye bags and wrinkles around the eyes
- For colour changes caused by fatigue

## Who Cannot Have Light Filler?

Filler and botox procedures are not recommended for pregnant or breastfeeding women, and this includes light filler. It is also not applied to people below a certain age.

## Why Is Under-Eye Filler Needed?

Many people are troubled by under-eye bags. Linked to genetic predisposition, the problem becomes more pronounced with ageing. One reason behind it is the loss of bone tissue that occurs over the years and is noticed only late.

The bones of the face also age and recede slightly, creating a hollow under the eyes. This millimetric loss often goes unnoticed, yet the shadows visible under the eyes in certain light become bothersome and make any dark circles look deeper and darker.

## Does Light Filler Have an Anti-Ageing Effect?

Yes. The vitamins, proteins, antioxidants and amino acids in the formula support the delicate skin of this area and make it more resilient to the effects of ageing.

## Can People in Their Twenties Have Light Filler?

Under-eye circles are unwelcome because they suggest a more tired appearance, particularly in working life. Patients of any age who are troubled by under-eye shadows can benefit from this treatment under physician supervision in a sterile environment.

## Does It Have Any Side Effects?

Under-eye light filler is injected with very fine needles and the product must be delivered slowly. The local anaesthetic content of the filler makes the treatment comfortable, and applying anaesthetic cream beforehand can make the area more comfortable still. Very mild swelling may appear after the procedure and resolves within a few days.

## What to Watch for After Light Filler

Redness rarely occurs at the injection points after treatment and disappears on its own within 3-4 hours. The injection points should not be rubbed during this time. No cream or cosmetic product should be applied to the face after the procedure. Your skin returns to normal after 4-5 hours.

## How Many Sessions Are Needed?

A single session is usually sufficient; in some cases 3 sessions at 3-week intervals may be recommended. The physician makes the final decision. Visible results are obtained from the first session.

## Does Under-Eye Light Filler Work?

In addition to factors such as age and gender, if the structure around your eyes is suitable for light filler, this treatment addresses under-eye shadows. Better results are achieved in patients with mild hollowing under the eyes; in those without hollowing, the area may take on a puffy appearance.

## How Much Filler Is Applied?

A volume of 0.5 cc to 1 cc is usually sufficient for most patients. Excessive dosing can leave the eye area looking fuller than normal. An additional dose 1-2 weeks later may be recommended where needed.

## Does It Hurt?

Thanks to the local anaesthetic cream applied to the skin surface beforehand, the procedure is completed without pain.

## Light Filler or Mesotherapy?

The results of under-eye light filler last around a year on average, depending on age, gender and the structure around the eyes. However, if there is no hollowing under your eyes, mesotherapy may be a more appropriate option than filler. Dr. Murat Irmak Clinic will recommend the most suitable treatment after examination.

## Who Should Not Have It?

It is not recommended during a flu-like infection. It is never applied to children under 12. It is also not recommended for people who are sensitive to any of the components of the filler material.

## How Long Does Recovery Take?

Because the under-eye area requires delicate work, the filler is delivered with a special cannula to avoid bruising. Slight redness or, rarely, mild bruising may occur after the procedure. Everyone recovers completely within three days at most.

## How Does Under-Eye Swelling Resolve?

Reducing alcohol, tea, coffee and salt before bed and getting 7-8 hours of restful sleep are important. Circular ice massage around the eyes can help relieve any swelling.

## Can Under-Eye Light Treatment Cause Blindness?

No such risk is expected in approved procedures performed by a competent physician under appropriate conditions.

## What Helps Hollow Eyes?

The most effective treatment for hollowing and dark circles under the eyes is under-eye light filler. Hyaluronic-acid-based light filler designed specifically for the under-eye area lasts approximately 10 to 18 months. If there is hollowing in your cheeks, filler injection can be applied there as well.

## When Does Under-Eye Filler Dissolve?

The hyaluronic acid formula is injected with very fine needles. Results can be seen immediately after application, and bruising and swelling are rare. The clearest result appears after a week. In some people the filler lasts 1 to 1.5 years.

## Is Under-Eye Light Filler Available at State Hospitals?

Under-eye light filler is one of the aesthetic procedures most frequently requested today by both men and women. It is not performed at state hospitals.
