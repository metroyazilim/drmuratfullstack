## Why Is the Under-Eye Area Different?

The under-eye area has some of the thinnest skin on the face. Its appearance is affected at the same time by several factors: skin thickness, the distribution of underlying fat tissue, bone structure and the visibility of blood vessels.

## What Is Assessed?

Before treatment, the anatomy of the area, the degree of existing hollowing and skin quality are examined together. Because the same appearance can have different causes, the cause needs to be identified before moving to treatment.

## How Is It Planned?

If considered appropriate, the plan is drawn up individually. Because the area is delicate, treatment should be performed by an experienced physician under clinical conditions.

## Follow-Up

Aftercare advice is shared and a check-up appointment is planned. If anything unexpected occurs, contacting the clinic is recommended.
