## What Is Botox?

Botox (botulinum toxin) is a purified substance that reduces muscle activity in a controlled way. It is used to soften the appearance of lines that become pronounced through repeated facial movement.

## How Is It Applied?

The procedure is performed by a physician under sterile clinic conditions. It is carried out with very fine needles and takes a few minutes. Applying cold before and after can help reduce redness.

## What Should You Watch For Afterwards?

It is recommended that the treated area not come into contact with water in the first hours. When washing your face or showering, avoid rubbing the area firmly. Staying out of the sun until swelling and redness subside is also among the recommendations.

## Follow-Up Appointment

There is a settling period after the procedure. A physician check-up is planned after this period and the assessment is repeated if needed.
