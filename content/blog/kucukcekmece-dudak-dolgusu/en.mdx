## What Is Discussed Beforehand?

A preliminary consultation takes place before a lip filler procedure. Many factors are assessed during this consultation, starting with your detailed health status. What your expectations are, why you want the procedure and what you hope for from the result are discussed openly.

## Why Does Medical History Matter?

Your physician assesses pre-existing health conditions and risk factors in detail. You need to provide information about any allergies you have, operations you have undergone and all prescription or over-the-counter medicines you take, including herbal supplements.

## Examination and Measurement

Before the intervention, your health professional examines your face and takes detailed measurements. Photographs of your face and lips are taken for your medical record, with your consent.

## Age Limits

In some countries the use of filler products is subject to a minimum age. It is advisable to review the regulations that apply in your country together with your physician.
