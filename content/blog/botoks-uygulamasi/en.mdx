## The Purpose of the Treatment

The aim of botox treatment is to reduce excessive movement of the facial muscles in a controlled way. This is intended to soften the appearance of lines associated with repeated facial expressions.

## Physician Assessment

A facial analysis is carried out before treatment; the physician determines which areas and which doses are appropriate. The same plan is not applied to everyone.

## Steps of the Procedure

The treatment is carried out by injecting small doses into points determined by the physician. The procedure is performed under sterile conditions and takes a short time.

## Follow-Up

There is a settling period after the treatment. A check-up appointment is planned once this period has passed, and the assessment is repeated if needed.
