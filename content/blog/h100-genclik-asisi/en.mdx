## What Is the Skin Booster Approach?

Treatments referred to as skin boosters are assessed among approaches aimed at skin quality and hydration support. The aim is to support the quality of the skin itself rather than to add volume.

## Who Is It Considered For?

Suitability varies from person to person. Skin type, current skin conditions, expectations and medical history are assessed together by the physician.

## How Is It Planned?

The number and spacing of sessions are not standard; they are determined individually after examination. The treatment is considered as part of an overall skin care plan.

## Please Note

This content is prepared for general information. A physician assessment is required to establish individual suitability.
