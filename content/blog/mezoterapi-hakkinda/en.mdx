## What Is Used in Mesotherapy?

Mixtures are prepared from components such as hyaluronic acid, antioxidants, vitamins, minerals and amino acids, according to the skin concern. The content of the mixture is not standard; it is determined by the problem being addressed.

## Why Micro-Injection?

The aim is to deliver active ingredients directly into the middle layer of the skin. This approach seeks to keep the substances in the target area and to achieve a different distribution compared with superficial application.

## How Is the Session Plan Built?

The number and spacing of sessions vary according to the needs of the skin, the goal and the protocol used. The final plan is drawn up by the physician after examination.

## Its Place in a Care Protocol

Mesotherapy is assessed more consistently when it is considered as part of an overall skin care plan rather than on its own.
